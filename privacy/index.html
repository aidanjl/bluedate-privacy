BlueDate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026 · Operator: Aidan Lawn

This policy describes what BlueDate collects, how it is used, and what stays on your device. It is written for App Store review and end users. It is not legal advice.

1. Summary

BlueDate helps adults find other BlueDate users nearby using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Today, profiles you see nearby, smiles, matches, chats, and party messages are handled on your device and over Bluetooth between phones. We do not currently operate a remote chat service that continues after you leave Bluetooth range.

You must be 21 or older. Age is confirmed by self-attestation in the app (you confirm you are 21+), not by government ID or date-of-birth verification.

2. Information the app uses

2.1 Profile information (on your device)

When you set up BlueDate, you may provide:

  • Display name
  • Short bio
  • Sex / interested-in preferences
  • Prompt answers
  • “Up for” activity tags
  • A selfie / profile photo (camera)

This information is stored locally on your device and, when you turn Discoverable on, is advertised to other nearby BlueDate users over Bluetooth so they can see your card.

2.2 Nearby discovery (Bluetooth)

With Discoverable on, BlueDate uses Bluetooth to:

  • Advertise a compact version of your profile (and optionally a small selfie thumbnail) to nearby devices running BlueDate
  • Scan for other BlueDate users nearby and show their profiles

Bluetooth identifiers and signal strength may be used on-device to estimate proximity. Discovery does not require an internet connection.

2.3 Smiles, matches, blocks, and chats

Smiles, matches, unmatches, local blocks, chat messages, and party-channel messages are stored on your device. Chat and party traffic between matched or nearby users is sent over Bluetooth while devices stay in range and the app can keep a link. If you leave range, hide yourself, or unmatch, those Bluetooth conversations stop. We do not promise cloud-backed or remote chat at this time.

2.4 Safety actions

You can hide yourself, unmatch, block locally, wipe local social data, and report another user. Reports may be sent:

  • To our API when a backend is configured in the app, and/or
  • By email to our support address when the API is not configured

Report contents may include the reported user’s local identifier, a reason you choose, and related context needed to investigate.

2.5 Device and local identifiers

BlueDate creates a local user ID on your device (stored in app preferences) so your profile and nearby sessions stay consistent. This is not a Sign in with Apple or Cognito account in the current shipping build.

2.6 Camera

The camera is used only so you can capture a selfie for your profile. Photos stay on device unless you later use an optional cloud sync feature (see below).

3. Optional / future cloud features

BlueDate’s product includes an AWS-backed API (profiles, photo upload, reports/blocks) that is not required for nearby Bluetooth use. If and when cloud configuration is enabled in a future build:

  • Profile sync and photo upload may send profile fields and images to our servers
  • Reports / blocks may be stored server-side to improve safety
  • Metrics / analytics may be collected to understand reliability and improve the product

We will update this policy and App Store privacy labels before enabling collection that changes the nutrition labels. Until cloud sync is turned on for your build, those server features are inactive.

Remote continue-chat after you leave the venue is a possible future feature. It is not part of the current product promise. If we add it, we will require clear consent and update this policy.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide nearby discovery, smiles, matches, and Bluetooth chat/party
  • Show your profile to other nearby users you choose to be discoverable to
  • Enforce safety tools (hide, block, report, wipe)
  • Comply with law and App Store requirements
  • (Future) operate optional sync, metrics, and support tooling when enabled

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

  • Other nearby users: When Discoverable is on, people using BlueDate nearby can see the profile information you advertise over Bluetooth.
  • Service providers (future): If cloud features are enabled, processors such as Amazon Web Services may host data under our instruction.
  • Legal / safety: We may retain and disclose report information if required by law or to address abuse.

We do not operate a public social feed or web profile directory in the current app.

6. Retention and your controls

  • Local profile, chats, matches, and blocks remain on your device until you delete them (e.g. Full wipe for social data, or deleting the app).
  • Deleting the app removes on-device BlueDate data.
  • Server-side retention (if cloud features are enabled later) will be limited to what is needed for the feature, safety, and legal obligations; details will be updated here when live.

In-app controls today include: Hide me, Unmatch, Block (local), Report, Full wipe and clear cache, and removing your selfie.

7. Children and age

BlueDate is for users 21 years of age and older. We do not knowingly allow use by anyone under 21. Age is based on your confirmation in the app. If you believe someone under 21 is using BlueDate, contact us using the support details below.

8. Security

We design the nearby experience to work without sending chats to our servers. No method of electronic storage or Bluetooth transmission is perfectly secure. Meet in public places and use Hide me / Block / Report if something feels wrong.

9. International users

BlueDate is initially offered via the Apple App Store. Processing (when cloud features exist) may occur in the regions where our providers operate (for example, AWS regions we configure).

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes (especially if cloud sync, metrics, or remote chat ship). The “Last updated” date will change, and material changes will be reflected in App Store privacy disclosures as required.
